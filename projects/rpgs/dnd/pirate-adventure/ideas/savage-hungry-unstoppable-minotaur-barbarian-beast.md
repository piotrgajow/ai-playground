# Minotaur – Barbarian (Path of the Beast)

*Savage, hungry, unstoppable* · Level 5 · D&D 5e (2014)

> A pit fighter shipped in chains to a new owner, who broke out of the hold halfway there and has sailed with the survivors ever since.

## Background

The character grew up fighting in the arena pits of a slaver port, sold from one owner to the next and fed only when they won. Their latest owner, Madame Serrat of the Crimson Pit, paid a fortune for them and had them chained in the hold of the merchant cog *Gilded Heifer* for the trip. Three days out, pirates boarded the cog. In the chaos the character tore their shackles out of the hull and killed everyone between them and fresh air, sailors and pirates alike. When it was over, the pirate captain stood in front of the blood-soaked minotaur and offered the only thing that would have worked: a hot meal and a share of the loot. The character has been with the crew ever since. **Hook/secret for the GM:** a brand on the character's shoulder shows Serrat's mark. Serrat hasn't given up on her property, and bounty hunters carry the brand's likeness. The character also knows exactly where the pit is (they always know which way is north) and has never decided whether to burn it down or never go near it again.

## Playstyle

The character is the party's wall and wrecking ball. They rage on turn one and charge in with **Goring Rush** (Dash, then a horn attack as a bonus action), soaking weapon hits through resistance and d12 hit points. From then on they switch their Beast form to suit the fight:

- **Claws:** three attacks per turn, each with the rage bonus, to shred the crew.
- **Bite:** heals themselves when they're low.
- **Tail:** reach and a reaction that boosts AC to guard the necromancer.

Their **signature move** is **Hammering Horns**: after landing a hit, they shove the enemy 10 ft with their horns as a bonus action, straight over the rail and into the sea. Outside combat they haul cargo and scare dockworkers with Intimidation. While raging they climb the rigging with advantage on Strength checks. They also always know where north is, which impresses navigators.

## Vibe

Few words, all of them literal. The character thinks in simple terms (fight, eat, sleep, loot) and finds human politeness baffling. Food is the center of their world: they judge people by whether they share it, remember every meal they've been given, and mean it when they say they're hungry after a fight. They aren't stupid, only direct; they treat freedom as the most precious thing they own and react to chains, cages or orders shouted in a certain tone with barely controlled fury.

- *"You talk a lot. Do you have food?"*
- *"Chains. Hate chains." (said while ripping a cage door off its hinges)*
- *"Captain says don't eat the prisoners. Captain is very strict."*

## Visual description

Nearly seven feet tall, with a heavily muscled body covered in dark brown fur. The broad bull's head has one horn capped with a scavenged iron tip and the other cracked from an old arena fight. They wear no armor: a sailcloth kilt, a wide leather belt with handaxes and javelins, and the broken shackles still riveted to both wrists, kept as trophies. There's a brass ring through the nose and Serrat's burn-scar brand on one shoulder. When they rage, the fingers split into black talons, the jaw widens, and sometimes a lashing tail forms behind them.

## Personality

- **Traits:** blunt and literal, always hungry, fiercely loyal to anyone who has fed them.
- **Ideal:** freedom. Nobody owns anyone, and anyone who tries deserves the horns.
- **Bond:** the pirate crew that freed them, and the unfinished business with the Crimson Pit.
- **Flaw:** they lose control around chains and slavers, and will break any plan to get at one.
- **With the party:**
  - They like the **swashbuckler**, who is fast, fun and always has snacks. They don't understand the showing off.
  - The **necromancer** makes them deeply uneasy, since the undead don't eat or sleep. Still, they accept that the skeletons carry heavy things.
  - They love the **bard**'s music and will demand songs about their own fights.

## Build notes

- **Race:** Minotaur (Monsters of the Multiverse), with +2 Strength and +1 Constitution.
- **Stats:** point buy STR 15, DEX 14, CON 14, INT 8, WIS 12, CHA 8, which becomes **STR 17, CON 15** after racial bonuses. At level 4 take +1 Strength and +1 Constitution for **STR 18, DEX 14, CON 16**.
  - Unarmored AC is 10 + 2 + 3 = **15**.
  - Average HP at level 5 is **55**.
- **Beast forms (a new choice each rage):**
  - **Claws:** best damage. You get 3 attacks per Attack action (1d6 + 4 + 2 rage each). The extra claw attack is part of the Attack action, not a bonus action, so it doesn't conflict with Hammering Horns.
  - **Bite:** use it when a fight is going to be long. Each bite that deals damage heals you by +3 while you're below half HP.
  - **Tail:** use it when you need reach or to protect yourself (+1d8 AC as a reaction).
- **Minotaur bonus actions:**
  - **Goring Rush:** Dash at least 20 ft, then make a horn attack (1d6 + Str) as a bonus action. Use it to close the gap on turn 2 or later.
  - **Hammering Horns:** after a melee hit made with the Attack action, shove the target 10 ft as a bonus action (Str save DC 15). On a ship, this pushes enemies overboard.
- **Skills:**
  - Barbarian: Athletics, Intimidation.
  - Primal Knowledge (level 3): Perception.
  - Background (Entertainer, Gladiator variant): Acrobatics, Performance.
- **Equipment:** 4 javelins and 2 handaxes for ranged attacks, an explorer's pack and a grappling hook. Keep a greataxe as a backup in case you can't rage.
- **Rage:** 3 per long rest, +2 damage.
  - While raging you have advantage on Strength checks, which covers climbing, swimming, grappling and shoves.
  - Reckless Attack gives you advantage on your attacks.
- **Turn 1:** Rage uses your bonus action, so Hammering Horns and Goring Rush can't be used on the same turn.
- **Traps to avoid:**
  - Rage ends early if you don't attack or take damage during a turn, so don't hang back.
  - You can't cast spells or concentrate while raging.
  - Don't carry a shield. Claws turn your hands into talons, so how a shield works with them is a GM ruling, and it isn't worth the argument.
  - Bestial Soul (level 6, which gives swimming and climbing speeds) isn't available at level 5.
  - Reckless Attack also gives enemies advantage against you. With AC 15, only use it when you have rage resistance up.
