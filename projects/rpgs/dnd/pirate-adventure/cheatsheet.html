Rules reference

Concentration.

One concentration spell at a time – casting another ends the first. When you take damage: CON save, DC 10 or half the damage (whichever is higher), one save per source; you roll +2 with advantage. Ends if you are incapacitated. A wave crashing over you on a storm-tossed ship may call for a DC 10 save. Yours: Spirit Guardians, Crusader's Mantle, Bless, Hold Person, Shield of Faith, Divine Favor, Magic Weapon, Guidance.

Bonus-action spells.

If you cast a spell as a bonus action, the only other spell you can cast that turn is a cantrip with a 1-action casting time. Healing Word + Toll the Dead is fine; Healing Word + Guiding Bolt, or Spirit Guardians + Spiritual Weapon, is not. Spiritual Weapon + Attack is fine.

Components & focus.

A holy symbol borne on your shield replaces material components that have no cost. A component with a cost must be the real thing, and a consumed one is needed for every casting (Revivify – 300 gp diamond).

Opportunity attacks.

A hostile creature you can see moves out of your reach → use your reaction for one melee attack just before it leaves. Not provoked by Disengage, teleporting or being moved without using its movement, action or reaction (shoved, thrown, falling).

Grappling.

Replaces an attack of the Attack action. Target at most one size larger and in reach; needs a free hand (put away the warhammer first). Athletics +6 vs its Athletics or Acrobatics. Grappled: speed 0. You can drag it at half speed. It escapes with its action by winning the same contest against your Athletics.

Shoving.

Replaces an attack of the Attack action; no free hand needed. Athletics +6 vs its Athletics or Acrobatics. Win → prone (melee attacks within 5 ft have advantage against it, it has disadvantage on attacks) or push 5 ft (over a railing?).

Death saving throws.

At 0 HP you fall unconscious. At the start of each turn roll d20: 10+ success, lower failure; 3 successes → stable, 3 failures → dead. Natural 1 = two failures, natural 20 = regain 1 HP. Damage at 0 HP = one failure (critical hit: two). Leftover damage ≥ your HP max (38) kills outright. Any healing wakes you. Stabilise an ally: action, DC 10 WIS (Medicine), you roll +4.
